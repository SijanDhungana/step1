About Life With Green Lighting

Electrician Toronto: 30 Years of Keeping GTA Homes Safe

I still remember the call I got from a homeowner in Leslieville on a January evening. Her kitchen had gone dark mid-dinner, the breaker kept tripping, and she had no idea why. When I got there, I found aluminum wiring that had been quietly overheating behind her walls for years. Nobody had ever told her it was a problem. That night changed how I think about this work.

After more than 30 years working across Toronto and the Greater Toronto Area, from Etobicoke to Pickering, from Markham to Mississauga, I have seen what happens when electrical systems are ignored or patched together by the wrong hands. What we do at Life with Green Lighting is treat every job the way I would want someone to treat mine: honest pricing, appointments that respect your schedule, and technicians who actually explain what they found.

Lighting Contractor Toronto

The Right Light Changes Everything

I worked on a home in Forest Hill where the owner had spent significantly on furniture and finishes, but the lighting was a scattered mix of old pot lights and a single overhead fixture that cast the whole space in a flat, unflattering glow. After a full lighting redesign, LED pot lights throughout the main floor, a pendant over the kitchen island, and a dimmer on the dining room circuit, she told me it felt like a different house. Same furniture. Same paint. Completely different feeling.

That is what good lighting does. It is not just about visibility. It shapes how a space feels to the people living in it.

EV Charger Installation Toronto

I drive electric myself. The first thing I did before picking up the car was have a Level 2 charger installed in my garage, because I knew relying on a standard 120 volt outlet to charge an electric vehicle is like trying to fill a bathtub with a garden hose. It works, technically, but it is not how you want to live.

We get calls from Oakville, Richmond Hill, Thornhill, and all across Toronto from people who just took delivery of a Tesla or Hyundai Ioniq and want to know what it takes to charge properly at home. A lot of homes in older Toronto neighbourhoods, the Danforth, St. Clair West, East York, have panels never sized for the additional load. We assess your setup, tell you exactly what needs to happen, and give you a clear price before any work begins.
